## About me

I’m Xiaotong “Kary” Fang, a PhD candidate in Computational Neuroscience at the University of Southern California working with [Payam Piray](https://www.piraylab.com/). My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning, computational neuroscience, and decision science**, with a focus on understanding how people and models learn under uncertainty.

I build statistical and deep learning models for sequential data, including Hidden Markov Models, Kalman filters, particle filters, Bayesian models, and recurrent neural networks. My research focuses on extracting latent structure from noisy behavior, quantifying uncertainty in model predictions, and understanding how learning changes across environments and individuals.

### What I work on

- **Sequential and latent-state modeling** — inferring hidden structure from behavioral time series.
- **Uncertainty quantification** — understanding model confidence, calibration, and different sources of predictive uncertainty.
- **Deep learning for behavioral data** — training and evaluating RNN-based models for sequential prediction and representation learning.
- **Multimodal modeling** — combining behavior with pupillometry, eye tracking, and other physiological signals.
- **Causal and experimental methods** — designing experiments and models that help distinguish competing explanations of observed behavior.

### Recent highlights

At **Google**, I worked as a Business Data Scientist Intern on **uncertainty quantification** for a large-scale **causal inference** system. The project involved more than **20M advertiser time-series records**, empirical model benchmarking, Bayesian neural network components, Monte Carlo inference, and integration into an existing ML pipeline.

My recent academic work includes publications and manuscripts on computational learning and neural modeling, including work in **Psychological Review**. I am also developing new sequence models for learning and decision-making across human and animal behavior.

### Industry interests

I’m interested in machine learning and data science roles involving **large-scale sequential data, deep learning, model evaluation, transfer learning, uncertainty quantification, causal inference, and robust ML systems**.

I’m particularly drawn to teams working on complex real-world systems where model quality depends not only on predictive accuracy, but also on careful experimentation, calibration, failure analysis, and reliable evaluation.
